# Milestone 1: Community notes

**Community:** r/PharmacyTechnician (public subreddit, about 62k members)
**Why:** I hold a CPhT, so I understand the posts and can label them consistently. No spoilers, and patient questions are not allowed there.

## Candidate distinctions I saw

1. **Some posts ask for something; others don't.** Question posts ask for an answer or advice (for example, why it is so hard to get a pharmacy tech job). Rants ask nothing.
2. **Some posts are about feelings; others give usable information.** A rant tells a story about something frustrating at work. A tips post explains how a task or role works (for example, being a med rec tech in a hospital).
3. **Some posts celebrate a milestone,** such as passing the certification exam. They share good news and usually ask nothing.
4. **Some posts include specifics** (a state, a pay rate, an exam name, years of experience) and others are general.

## Note
Many posts carry an author-chosen tag (Rant, Question, Tips & Tricks). I won't copy the tag as my label, because authors tag inconsistently, but it is a useful check.
